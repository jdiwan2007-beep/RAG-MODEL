Card Data Handling SOP (PCI-DSS)

Agents must never ask for, record or repeat full card numbers, CVV or PIN, in chat, email or call notes. If a customer shares card details, tell them not to, and delete the message where possible. Only the last 4 digits of a card may be referenced. Payments are completed only on the secure checkout page.
