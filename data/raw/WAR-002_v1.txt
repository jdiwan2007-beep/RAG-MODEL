Warranty Claim SOP

Step 1: Ask for the invoice and serial number. Step 2: Confirm the product is within warranty. Step 3: Run the basic troubleshooting checklist. Step 4: If unresolved, register a claim and send the item to the authorised service centre. Step 5: Repairs take 7 to 14 working days; inform the customer of the ticket number.
