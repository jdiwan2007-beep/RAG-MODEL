EMI Policy

No-cost EMI is available on purchases of Rs 10,000 and above for select credit cards, with tenures of 3, 6 and 9 months. Debit card EMI is available for purchases above Rs 15,000. A processing fee of Rs 199 applies to debit card EMI.
